## Direction artistique commune

Wide 16:9 landscape Dungeons & Dragons sourcebook illustration, painterly realistic fantasy, restrained visible oil-painted brushwork, rich natural textures, believable anatomy, cinematic depth and lighting, elegant detailed environments, atmospheric and emotionally grounded. Match the established character portraits and moonlit city references. No text, letters, labels, captions, watermark, signature, UI, frame or collage. No modern equipment.
