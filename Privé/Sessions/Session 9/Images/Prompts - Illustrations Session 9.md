# Prompts — Illustrations de la Session 9

Les images ont été produites avec le générateur d’images intégré, dans un style commun de **fantasy réaliste peinte et cinématographique**.

## Rencontre avec Olo

Références : `Nyxara.png`, `Orla.png`, `Josué.png`, `Zooki.png`, `Buffo.png`.

> Create a wide landscape cinematic fantasy illustration for a tabletop RPG scene, painterly realism with rich natural textures and dramatic but warm woodland light. Preserve the identities, faces, hair, clothing motifs, body proportions and relative scale of Nyxara, Orla, Josué, Zooki and Buffo from the reference portraits. The exhausted adventurers enter Olo’s tiny primitive threshold hut, made of woven branches, leaves, roots and dried mud. Inside, the cramped room overflows with books. Olo is an elderly Tortle with a moss-covered shell, threadbare robe and tiny round spectacles, seated behind a desk far too small for him, his feet in a basin of warm water. He keeps reading and raises one finger without looking up. Nyxara, Orla, Josué and Zooki stand in the doorway; beside them is Maro, a small elderly traveler with exceptionally long white hair and beard in a brown monk robe, while enormous mushroom-backed Buffo remains outside. Hint at Olo’s impossible power only through motionless water droplets, faint concentric shell runes continuing into the roots, and an almost imperceptible translucent giant tortoise silhouette. No combat, duplicated characters, typography, captions, signs, watermark, UI or border.

## Le Puits des Songes — découverte

> Create a wide landscape cinematic fantasy environment concept painting: an awe-inspiring high bird’s-eye discovery view of Le Puits des Songes from a circular whitewood elevator platform descending through mist. Travelers expected a primitive village and discover a magnificent city of several thousand inhabitants inside an impossibly deep forest sinkhole. Far below lies the Grand Mirror, a luminous silver-blue moonwater lake covered in green-silver Kaluphare lily pads and white glowing flowers. Thousands of elegant homes, palaces, workshops and gardens rise above it on colossal living stilts and braided roots. Show luminous shell-and-leaf facades, suspended root bridges, inverted gardens, floating plazas, monumental translucent art veils, airborne water channels and waterfalls flowing upward. Tiny water Genasi and symbiotic moon-mantas, glass spiders, bell-toads and sap-otters establish scale. Extreme high angle, platform and traveler silhouettes in the upper foreground, mist opening like curtains, immense vertical depth, ancient forest around the rim, twilight blue, emerald and silver palette with warm windows. Magical living city, not ruins, rustic swamp village or science fiction. No typography, captions, labels, watermark, UI or border.

## Nyxara — forme lupine séléenne

Référence : `Nyxara.png`.

> Create a vertical full-body fantasy character concept portrait of Nyxara in her Selenian lupine form. Preserve and translate her identity into an anthropomorphic female werewolf: vivid yellow-green eyes, long dark dreadlocks becoming a heavy dreadlock-like mane, distinctive vertical silver-white markings from forehead across the eyes and down the muzzle and cheeks, and recognizable remnants of dark brown fur-and-leather forest clothing. She is upright, Medium-sized but powerfully built, with massive shoulders, muscular limbs, dense charcoal-black fur, long claws, a strong lupine muzzle and pointed ears. She looks like a brutal frontline protector with exceptional Strength and Constitution, controlled and intelligent rather than feral. Her grounded stance is ready to intercept a blow meant for an ally. She cannot speak or cast magic, so show no spellcasting, weapon, staff, magical hand effect or armor. Convey her lunar nature only through silver rain, wet fur, crescent reflections and pale moonlight in a dark Selenian forest. Painterly cinematic fantasy realism, clear silhouette, natural anatomy, one character, full body visible. No blood, gore, extra wolves, extra limbs, typography, captions, watermark, UI or border.

## Veillée du Reflet

Références : `Nyxara.png`, `Orla.png`, `Josué.png`, `Zooki.png`. Le premier rendu a été corrigé afin de faire apparaître exactement sept anciens.

> Create a wide landscape cinematic fantasy ritual scene on a living-root terrace above the luminous Puits des Songes. Preserve Nyxara, Orla, Josué and Zooki from the reference portraits. Nyxara is alone inside a shallow circular moonwater pool in her clearly anthropomorphic female wolf form: charcoal fur, powerful build, dreadlock-like mane, yellow-green eyes and vertical silver-white facial markings. She is calm, vulnerable and wakefully dreaming among glowing white Kaluphare flowers. Exactly seven elderly water Genasi sages form a loose circle: three on the left, three on the right and one centered at the far side below the witnesses. No throne, ruler or crowns. Each elder has one distinct linked symbiotic animal nearby: two bell-toads, one small moon-manta, one glass spider, one sap-otter, one luminous salamander and one small amphibious forest creature. Orla, Josué and Zooki stand together as exactly three witnesses in the upper background. Show suspended root bridges, upward-flowing waterfalls, luminous palaces and translucent art veils beyond. Sacred, intimate and wondrous; strong circular composition; deep blue-silver palette; painterly high-end tabletop RPG art. No additional people, duplicated heroes, typography, captions, watermark, UI or border.
